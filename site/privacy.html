Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking. The only thing the app ever sends about you is a result report, and only when you click Send after reading it.

Who is responsible

Riccardo Lardi, who publishes shim-dlss5 as a private, open-source project. Contact for anything on this page, including deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub or send a private message via Ko-fi.

The app

shim-dlss5 runs on your PC. It reads your game launchers' records and your game folders to decide what it can install, and keeps its own files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\shim. None of that leaves your PC.

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • download the components you choose to fetch, from their publishers (GitHub, reshade.me);
  • fetch game cover images from Steam's image server, for Steam games without a cached cover;
  • check GitHub for a newer release, if "Check for updates" is on in Settings;
  • send a result report, only when you click Send (below).

Those services see your IP address as part of any internet request, under their own privacy policies. The app sends them no information about you beyond the request itself.

Result reports

On an installed game's page, "Share this result" shows the exact record before anything is sent. A report contains:

Your answerworks / no visible effect / crashes
Up to five lines from the component logwith every file path cut down to the file name
App version0.2.0

It contains no name, account, machine id, file path, Windows user name, or hash of your model file. Reports are anonymous: they cannot be linked to you or to each other, which also means we cannot find "your" report later. If you sent something you regret, contact us with the game and the date and we will delete matching reports.

Reports are stored in a Postgres database (Neon, hosted for us through Vercel) and shown in aggregate on the games pages once a game has two reports. They are kept as long as the games pages exist.

Rate limiting. To stop flooding, the server computes a keyed hash of your IP address combined with the current date and keeps a counter under that hash for at most one day. The IP address itself is not stored with the report or anywhere else by us, and the hash changes every day, so it cannot be used to recognise you across days.

Legal basis (GDPR and the Swiss FADP): your consent, given each time you click Send, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service usable for the rate limiting.

This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics or advertising. It is hosted by Vercel, whose servers log requests (including IP addresses) for security and operations for a limited time under Vercel's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them (Google's privacy policy). Links to GitHub and Ko-fi take you to those services under their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of any data about you, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Since reports are anonymous, the most useful thing you can send is the game and date of a report you want removed.

Changes

If what the app sends ever changes, this page and the preview in the app change with it, before a release ships the new behaviour.
